# Weathering Design Challenge

A single-file engineering design challenge for Grade 7 STEAM, Explore the Land, Lesson 4 Extension. It replaces a Jamboard extension activity that no longer works.

The challenge: design and create a protective covering for a glass and metal wind chime so it can stay outside through winter. Students design against a five-point criteria checklist (snow, ice, and rain; small rocks and sand in high winds; the chime swinging against the house; recycled or repurposed materials only; a fastening system that comes off and goes back on easily), brainstorm and sketch on a drawing canvas, list their materials, show a prototype as a photo (read locally in the browser only) or a labelled diagram, answer the board's two reflection questions, rate their design from 1 to 5, and record a real-world test of their design. A design plan compiles everything with Print and Copy buttons.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
