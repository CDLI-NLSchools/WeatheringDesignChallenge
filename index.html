Weathering Design Challenge

Grade 7 STEAM, Explore the Land, Lesson 4 Extension

An engineering design challenge: protect a wind chime from a winter of weathering, using only materials you already have at home.

Imagine

The end of summer is approaching. You gaze at the beautiful metal and glass wind chime you spent many warm evenings listening to, when suddenly a thought pops into your head.

What if your beloved wind chime experiences the effects of weathering, like the rocks you recently learned about? You could take it in for the winter, but it was so much work attaching it to a wire high up on the eave of your house.

Could you design and create a protective case to cover it?

Your challenge
